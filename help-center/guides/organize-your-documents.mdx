## Quick Guide

Follow these steps to keep your documentation tidy:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Folder">
    ````tip
    Go to your dashboard, click "+ New Folder", and name it.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Documents">
    ````tip
    Inside the folder, click "+ New Document" to start writing.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange and Share">
    ````tip
    Drag documents to reorder and use the "Share" button for team access.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
